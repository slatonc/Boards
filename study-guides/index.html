ABIM study guides for internal medicine residents

Make your question-bank work easier to revisit. These guides focus on planning your study time, learning from missed questions, and turning a review book into a reference you can use.

  • Build a realistic ABIM study plan

    A flexible 12-week example, with a smaller routine for busy rotations and a weekly checkpoint that keeps your plan honest.

  • Review missed questions without rewriting the explanation

    Identify the decision you missed, write one useful note, and return to it later without the answer in view.

  • Free cardiology review sample

    Preview the printed format and practice separating recognition of a diagnosis from the next decision.

  • Free pulmonology review sample

    A way to organize pulmonary review around patterns, interpretation, and the clues that separate similar presentations.

  • Free nephrology review sample

    Use a compact set of prompts to make renal question review more deliberate and your notes easier to find.

    Why Slaton Case wrote For The Boards, how the book fits beside a question bank, and where to send corrections.
