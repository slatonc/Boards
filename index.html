Why it works

Presentation-first, not diagnosis-first

A board vignette never opens with the answer. It opens with a symptom, a vital sign, and a lab value. Books organized by diagnosis train your recall in the opposite direction from the way you’re tested. This one runs the way the question runs.

Built to sit next to your Qbank

Not a second curriculum. It’s built for the knowledge you already have, not to reteach medicine from scratch. When a question catches you out, find the scenario and write the lesson in the margin: the mnemonic, the next step, the trap you fell for.

By test day, it’s yours

Three years of margin notes turn it into a reference no one else has: highlighted, dog-eared, familiar because you wrote it. In the final weeks you’re not searching a database. You already know what page it’s on.

This book gets revised. Send corrections or additions to Slaton@ForTheBoards.com — if we use yours, you’re credited in the next edition and we donate $5 to Doctors Without Borders.

Each scenario resolves into labeled lines: Dx, Next, Contrast, Trap. No wall of textbook text to mine.
