Why it works

Presentation-first, not diagnosis-first

Boards and patients open with a presentation. The book starts with symptoms and clues, then connects them to the diagnosis—how to confirm it, treat it, monitor it, and separate look-alikes. Organized around the ABIM blueprint, without textbook bulk.

Built to sit next to your Qbank

Not a second curriculum. When a question teaches you something, find the card and write it in—mnemonics, next steps, traps—so the lesson sticks beside the high-yield scaffold.

Becomes your board book

Over residency it turns into a personalized ABIM reference: highlighted, annotated, and familiar because you built it—and you can hold it for final review.
