Processing is intentionally restrained because the recordings have strong headroom and a low measured noise floor. The chain uses end trimming, a 70 Hz high-pass filter, gentle low-mid reduction, light presence enhancement, mild de-essing, soft compression, and final two-pass loudness normalization.
